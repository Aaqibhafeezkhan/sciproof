# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This file is a short summary written for this project; the maintainers may replace it with the
full official text later.

## Our standard

We want a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience or career stage.

Encouraged: respectful and constructive feedback, welcoming newcomers, accepting that
people make mistakes and helping to fix them, focusing on what is best for the project and its
users.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal or political attacks, publishing
other people's private information, sustained disruption of discussions, and any conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge
inappropriate. Report problems to the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**; select it for conduct reports too), or by contacting a maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with respect for the privacy of the reporter.
